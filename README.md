# Unsent: The Micro-Confession Sprint

A two-part micro-confession for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-boothlord-unsent-the-micro-confession-sprint-8c6596ba): a `To:` line naming who the unsent message was meant for, and the unsent line itself, written exactly as it was typed — and never sent.

`main.py` (Python, stdlib only) writes the confession to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` as UTF-8 text: two parts, 201 characters total, no trailing newline.

> To: my grandma's neighbour, who still waters her plants
>
> i told you she was just visiting family, and i have let you keep watering a dead woman's plants for eight months because i cannot say it out loud
